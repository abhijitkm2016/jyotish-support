Jyotish — The Cosmic Sage

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

Overview

Jyotish — The Cosmic Sage (“Jyotish”, “the app”) is a guided reflection and learning experience. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and the controls available to you.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your name, birth profile, saved Kundli, game and learning progress, language, appearance, and practice preferences locally on your device. This information supports your personal experience and remains on the device unless a feature described below needs limited information to operate.

Astrology calculations

To calculate a verified Kundli, the app sends birth date, birth time, timezone, and birthplace coordinates securely to the Jyotish calculation service. The service uses Prokerala as its astrology calculation provider. Your name is not included in this request.

To calculate today’s Panchang and sky information, the app may send current coordinates and the calculation time. Location permission is optional. If you deny permission, location-dependent results may be unavailable or use a location you choose.

Account information

Creating an account is optional. If you sign in with Apple or Game Center, the Jyotish service stores the minimum provider identifier and session data required to authenticate and protect your account. A display name may be stored as an optional account label.

Optional purchases

When optional Prasad purchases are enabled, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store and purchase entitlement information is processed by RevenueCat. Jyotish does not receive your full payment-card details. Purchases are disabled in builds where the complete purchase and recovery flow is not available.

Expert guidance

If you use Expert Guidance, the Jyotish service stores your selected service, duration, topic, appointment time, Kundli-sharing choice, price, promotion status, and limited Razorpay payment identifiers needed to reserve and confirm the booking. Appointment holds expire after 15 minutes unless payment is confirmed. Razorpay processes UPI or card payment; Jyotish does not receive your full card details, CVV, UPI PIN, password, or one-time code.

Kundli information will be shared for a session only after you explicitly choose to share it. Booking and payment records may be retained as needed to deliver the appointment, provide support, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and meet accounting or legal obligations.

Advertising, sale, and tracking

The current release does not show third-party advertising and does not sell personal information. Birth information is not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

You can clear birth data or reset all local progress from Settings. You can delete your service account and linked sign-in identifiers from the Account screen.

Deleting the service account does not automatically erase birth data or game progress stored locally on your device. Use Clear Birth Data or Reset All Progress in Settings to remove those records. Deleting the app also removes its local data, subject to device backups managed by your platform account.

Security and children

The app uses encrypted network connections for remote calculations and account operations. No system can guarantee absolute security. The app is not designed to collect information from children without the involvement of a parent or guardian.

Changes

This policy may be updated as features or providers change. The effective date above will be changed when a material update is made.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to abhijitmishra.apps.support@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.
